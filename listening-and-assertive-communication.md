# Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

Active listening means listening to someone carefully and trying to understand what they are saying. Some strategies that I can follow to improve my active listening are:

1. **Stay Focused:** Give full attention to the person who is speaking. Avoid distractions like mobile phones or other things.
2. **Listen Carefully:** Try to understand what the other person is saying instead of thinking about what to reply.
3. **Don't Interrupt:** Let the other person finish speaking before giving your opinion.
4. **Show Interest:** Use words like "Yes", "Okay" and "I understand" to show that you are listening.
5. **Use Body Language:** Maintain eye contact, nod your head and show interest through your expressions.
6. **Ask Questions:** If something is not clear, ask questions to understand it better.
7. **Take Notes:** Write down important points during meetings or discussions so that you don't forget them.
8. **Repeat in Your Own Words:** Repeat the important points in your own words to check whether you understood them correctly.

# Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

Reflective listening means listening carefully and making sure that we understand the other person's message correctly. It also helps us understand their opinions and feelings.

The important points of reflective listening are:

1. **Listen Without Interrupting:** Allow the speaker to complete their thoughts before responding.
2. **Remember Important Points:** Try to remember the main information shared by the speaker.
3. **Take Notes:** Write down important details during meetings or discussions.
4. **Summarize the Message:** Repeat the main points in your own words to check your understanding.
5. **Keep an Open Mind:** Listen to others even when their opinions are different from yours.
6. **Avoid Negative Reactions:** Don't get angry or argue when someone has a different opinion.
7. **Show Interest:** Give proper attention to the speaker and try to understand their point of view.

These steps help us avoid misunderstandings and make communication better.

For example, instead of telling someone that their behaviour made me feel bad, I may stay silent or give short replies. Sometimes I may also use sarcastic comments to show that I am upset.

I know this is not a good way to handle situations because it can create misunderstandings. Instead, I should explain what is bothering me directly and politely.

# Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive?

Assertive communication means expressing our thoughts, feelings and opinions confidently while also respecting other people's feelings and opinions.

After watching the video, I understood that being assertive is important in both personal and professional life. I can improve my communication by following these steps:

1. **Express My Feelings Clearly:** I should explain what I feel instead of hiding my feelings or keeping everything inside.
2. **Understand My Needs:** Before speaking, I should understand what I actually need and explain it clearly to others.
3. **Start with Small Situations:** I can first practise assertive communication with my friends and family. This will help me become more confident.
4. **Maintain Proper Body Language:** I should maintain eye contact and speak in a normal tone instead of showing anger through my expressions or gestures.
5. **Speak at the Right Time:** If something bothers me, I should talk about it early instead of waiting until I become frustrated.
6. **Respect Other People's Opinions:** I should listen to others and respect their opinions even when they are different from mine.
7. **Learn to Say No:** I should learn to say no politely when I am not comfortable with something instead of agreeing to everything.

For example, instead of saying "You never give me time", I can say "I feel bad when we don't spend time together. Can we plan some time to talk?"

By following these steps, I can become more confident and express my thoughts without hurting others. I believe assertive communication will help me improve my relationships and also communicate better with my teammates at work.
