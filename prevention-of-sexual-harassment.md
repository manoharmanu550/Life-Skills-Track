# Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment means any unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe or disrespected. It can happen in three forms:

**1. Verbal Harassment:**
- Making unwanted comments about someone's dress, clothing or body structure.
- Making sexual jokes or comments about someone's behaviour.
- Spreading rumours about someone's sexual life.
- Using foul or obscene language.
- Making unwanted sexual remarks or asking for sexual favours.

**2. Visual Harassment:**
- Drawing or displaying obscene pictures, posters or cartoons of a sexual nature.
- Keeping inappropriate sexual images as screensavers or wallpapers.
- Sending sexual content through emails or text messages.
- Showing pornographic pictures or videos without someone's consent.

**3. Physical Harassment:**
- Sexual assault or unwanted physical contact.
- Blocking someone's way or stopping them from moving freely.
- Inappropriate touching, such as kissing, hugging, patting, stroking or rubbing without consent.
- Making unwanted sexual gestures.
- Leering or staring at someone in a sexual manner.

Sexual harassment can also be divided into two categories:

- **Quid Pro Quo (This for That):** This happens when someone offers job benefits, promotion or other advantages in return for sexual favours. It can also involve threatening someone's job if they refuse.
- **Hostile Work Environment:** This happens when unwanted sexual behaviour creates an uncomfortable, intimidating or offensive environment at the workplace.

**Question 2: What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?**

If I face or witness any such incident, I would not ignore it. I would take it seriously and follow these steps:

1. If someone behaves this way with me, I would ask them to stop if it is safe to do so.
2. If I witness such behaviour, I would support the affected person and check if they are okay.
3. I would note down the date, time, place and details of what happened.
4. I would save any useful evidence, such as messages, emails or screenshots.
5. I would report the incident to HR or the Internal Committee (IC/ICC), following the company's PoSH policy.
6. I would respect the affected person's privacy and avoid discussing the incident with unnecessary people.
7. If the behaviour continues, I would report it again and follow up with the concerned team.

I believe everyone should feel safe and respected at work. We should not stay silent when something wrong happens, and we should support people who face such situations.
