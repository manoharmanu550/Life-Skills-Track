### Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning where we understand a topic and explain it in simple words as if we are teaching it to someone.

### Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Focused Mode:** Focused mode means giving full attention to one particular task or topic. For example, when I sit and solve a Java programming problem without any distractions, I am using focused mode.

**Diffuse Mode:** Diffuse mode means allowing our mind to relax and think freely without concentrating on one particular thing. For example, when I take a short walk after studying a difficult topic, sometimes I get new ideas or understand things better.
